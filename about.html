For more than five decades, Oakridge has helped students find the confidence to think deeply, act generously, and make their mark.

Who we are

Known well. Challenged thoughtfully.

Oakridge International Academy is an independent, co-educational school serving learners from kindergarten through grade 12. In small classes, students are encouraged to take intellectual risks, learn from one another, and connect classroom ideas to the wider world.

Our faculty bring expertise and genuine care to their work. Together with families, they create an environment where achievement and belonging reinforce one another.
